DFM connects drifting generative modeling with flow-based transport. A two-time model learns to move between intermediate distributions, allowing the same model to generate in one step or refine its output over multiple steps.

The idea

Learn transport over an interval.

Flow Matching constructs a path from noise to data. DFM uses pairs of times along that path and a distribution-level drift objective to learn transport between their marginals.

During sampling, the model predicts a mean velocity over the chosen interval. A single interval gives direct generation; a sequence of intervals gives multi-step refinement.
